Technical Reading Guide

How to read the market-structure layer
Relative Strength

Compares the stock with Nifty Midcap. A positive RS vs 50D means the stock is leading the broader market.

RSI 14

Momentum gauge. 40-60 is often a healthy reset, above 70 can be extended, below 30 can be oversold.

Moving Averages

50DMA tracks medium-term trend. 200DMA tracks long-term structure. Above both is generally stronger.

Point & Figure

Filters noise and highlights breakouts or breakdowns. It is a structure check, not a standalone trade signal.
